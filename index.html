Official game rules

HOW TO PLAY

🎤 One non-playing host controls the device, prepares each photo, judges answers, recognizes the first valid “STEAL!” call, and advances the game.
1. Rotating TurnsEach team receives a primary turn in automatic rotation.
2. Choose Before ViewingThe active team chooses Easy, Normal, or Hard before the clue is shown.
3. Difficulty and PointsEasy shows the full clear picture for 1 point. Normal shows roughly half the face for 2. Hard shows a small detail for 3.
4. Primary AnswerAfter difficulty is selected, the team has 15 seconds and one final answer.
5. StealAfter an incorrect answer, the host announces “Steal is now open!” The first recognized team to raise a hand and shout “STEAL!” receives 10 seconds.
6. Same ClueThe stealing team sees the same difficulty and crop. A correct steal earns 1 point. Only one steal is allowed.
7. RevealThe full baby picture, participant name, optional fun fact, and round result are shown after judging.
8. WinningThe highest positive score wins. A shared highest score is a tie. If all scores are zero, there is no winner.
Fair play: Only the active or recognized stealing team may answer. The host’s decisions are final.
