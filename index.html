GAME RULES

🎤 One non-playing host controls the device, checks answers, manages steals, and advances every round.
1. Teams Take TurnsOne active team receives the main attempt. Turns rotate automatically after each picture.
2. Choose the RiskHard blur earns 3 points, Normal earns 2, and Easy earns 1.
3. One Photo, One GuessThe active team has the selected time and may lock only one final answer.
4. Host JudgesThe host marks the answer Correct or Incorrect. An exact full name may earn a 1-point bonus.
5. One Steal ChanceIf enabled, one other team gets 5 seconds to steal for 1 point after an incorrect answer.
6. Fair PlayOnly the active or stealing team may answer. Other teams must not shout or signal.
7. Reveal and RotateThe host reveals the picture, then advances to the next team and round.
8. WinningHighest positive score wins. Equal top scores create a tie. All zero scores mean No Winner.
Recommended: 1 host, 3 to 5 teams, and one shared phone, tablet, or laptop.
